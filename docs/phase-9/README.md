This folder contains objective viewport captures from the Phase 9 regression matrix. Each declared viewport has a hero and contact capture generated from the local Vite site after the responsive/fallback corrections.

## Objective checks

- Semantic content stays inside the viewport at all ten sizes.
- The contact transmission SVG stays inside its form stage in portrait and landscape cuts.
- Orientation changes retain the seven contact controls and a reachable submit action.
- Reduced effects and no-WebGL preserve scene content; failed Zestt media settles into its honest fallback.
- The 320px no-JavaScript path retains the complete reading order and direct contact path.

These captures are regression evidence, not visual approval. Phase 9 visual approval remains Aman’s decision.
