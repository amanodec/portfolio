# Phase 11 fresh-viewer feedback

Status: not collected in this environment. No viewer observations or approval
have been invented.

## Required session setup

Aman should define the fresh-viewer group and review method before sessions
begin. The same launch candidate should be used for every session, with no
coaching about the intended Signal metaphor. Record the viewport/device class,
effects mode, whether the viewer used a mouse, touch, or keyboard, and the
exact words used by the viewer where possible.

## Questions to ask every viewer

1. Who do you think this site is about?
2. Which projects do you remember after the complete journey?
3. Which interaction or transition do you remember?
4. What felt unclear or difficult to navigate?
5. What felt excessive, repetitive, or tiring?
6. Did the page make you understand what Aman can build and how he thinks?
7. What would you remove before launch?
